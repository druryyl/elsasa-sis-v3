## 2. Findings

### `FINDING-P5-S11-01` — Level 2 Dashboard Route Guard Mismatch Causes `/403 Forbidden` for Authorized Roles & Permissions

- **Severity:** MAJOR
- **Resolution Status:** RESOLVED (Iteration 1)
- **Description:**
  Architecture TD-05 (`DEC-006`, `GAP-006`) and the Level 1/Level 2 backend endpoints authorize access to each domain dashboard when the caller holds **either** an allowed domain view permission **or** an allowed domain role (excluding self-service `Student`/`Parent` roles). In `P5-S11`, [`routes.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/routes.ts#L58-L120) specifies **both** `meta.permission` and `meta.roles` on `/dashboard/students`, `/dashboard/academics`, `/dashboard/finance`, `/dashboard/teaching`, and `/dashboard/operations`, while [`guards.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/guards.ts#L46-L111) enforces `to.meta.permission` and `to.meta.roles` as two mandatory **AND** checks—and its `to.meta.permission` branch lacks role fallbacks for the new dashboard routes. Consequently, authorized users who see the links in `AppLayout.vue` (via `isNavAuthorized`, which uses OR semantics) and receive `IsAccessible = true` from `GET /api/dashboard/executive` are redirected to `/403 Forbidden` on navigation.
- **Evidence:**
  1. [`guards.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/guards.ts#L46-L111) checks `if (to.meta.permission)` (lines 46–104) and then separately checks `if (to.meta.roles ...)` (lines 107–111). Neither check yields if the other passes.
  2. Demo user `coordinator` (`Roles: ["AcademicCoordinator", "AcademicAuthority"]`, academics permissions only in [`LoginEndpoint.cs`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/backend/Elsasa.Sis.Api/Features/Auth/LoginEndpoint.cs#L136-L172)) is granted `IsAccessible = true` for `teaching` and `operations` in [`GetExecutiveDashboardHandler.cs`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/backend/Elsasa.Sis.Api/Features/Dashboard/GetExecutiveDashboard/GetExecutiveDashboardHandler.cs#L80-L118) and sees `"Teaching KPIs"` and `"Operations KPIs"` in [`AppLayout.vue`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/layouts/AppLayout.vue#L158-L180), yet navigating to `/dashboard/teaching` fails `guards.ts` lines 80–84 (which only fall back to `['TeachingAuthority', 'TeachingCoordinator', 'Admin']`, omitting `AcademicCoordinator`, `AcademicAuthority`, and `Teacher`) and navigating to `/dashboard/operations` fails `guards.ts` line 48 (no role fallback for `Permissions.Operations.Calendar.Manage`), redirecting both to `/403`.
  3. Demo user `supervisor` (`Roles: ["Teacher", "AcademicStaff"]`, [`LoginEndpoint.cs`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/backend/Elsasa.Sis.Api/Features/Auth/LoginEndpoint.cs#L101-L134)) sees `"Teaching KPIs"` in `AppLayout.vue` and receives `IsAccessible = true` for `teaching`, yet navigating to `/dashboard/teaching` redirects to `/403` at `guards.ts` lines 80–84.
  4. Staff users holding a domain view permission whose role is not in the route's `meta.roles` array (e.g., `OperationsStaff` with `Permissions.Operations.Calendar.Manage` as used in [`AppLayout.spec.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/layouts/__tests__/AppLayout.spec.ts#L235-L238), `StudentAdminOfficer` / `SchoolLeadership` with `Permissions.Students.Admission.View`, `FinanceAuthority` with `Permissions.Finance.Obligations.View`, or `Staff` with `Permissions.Teaching.Faculty.View`) pass `guards.ts` Step 2 and are then blocked at Step 3 (`guards.ts` lines 107–111) with `/403`, despite backend endpoints and `GetExecutiveDashboardHandler.cs` granting access.
  5. Role-only `Admin` (without `*` permission) or `AdmissionOfficer` / `FinanceOfficer` / `FinanceStaff` / `OperationsAuthority` without explicit permission strings are blocked at Step 2 (`guards.ts` lines 46–104) on `/dashboard/students`, `/dashboard/finance`, and `/dashboard/operations`.
  6. Note on self-service roles: `student.jonathan` (`Roles: ["Student"]`) holds `academics.participations.view` and `parent.jonathan` (`Roles: ["Parent"]`) holds `Permissions.Finance.Obligations.View` in [`LoginEndpoint.cs`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/backend/Elsasa.Sis.Api/Features/Auth/LoginEndpoint.cs#L174-L219), so `AppLayout.vue` (`isNavAuthorized`) must also avoid exposing `/dashboard/academics` and `/dashboard/finance` to `Student`/`Parent` while allowing authorized staff roles and permissions.
- **Required Correction:**
  1. Reconcile [`guards.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/guards.ts), [`routes.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/routes.ts), and [`AppLayout.vue`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/layouts/AppLayout.vue) so that every Level 2 dashboard route (`/dashboard/students`, `/dashboard/academics`, `/dashboard/finance`, `/dashboard/teaching`, `/dashboard/operations`) allows navigation for all roles and permissions permitted by Architecture TD-05 and [`GetExecutiveDashboardHandler.cs`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/backend/Elsasa.Sis.Api/Features/Dashboard/GetExecutiveDashboard/GetExecutiveDashboardHandler.cs#L24-L118) (including `AcademicCoordinator`, `AcademicAuthority`, `Teacher` on `/dashboard/teaching`; `AcademicCoordinator`, `OperationsAuthority`, `OperationsStaff` with permission on `/dashboard/operations`; `Admin` without `*`; and permission-bearing staff like `StudentAdminOfficer`/`SchoolLeadership`/`FinanceAuthority`) without false `/403` redirects, while keeping unauthorized users (including `Guest`, `Student`, `Parent`, and unrelated domain staff) blocked from `/dashboard/{domain}` and hidden in the sidebar.
  2. Preserve all existing navigation guard semantics for non-dashboard routes (verified by `guards.spec.ts`), and add unit tests covering Level 2 dashboard route authorization (allowed roles, allowed permissions, and denied roles such as `Guest` / `Student` / `Parent` / cross-domain roles).
- **Remediation Evidence (Iteration 1):**
  - Created [`dashboardAccess.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/dashboardAccess.ts#L28-L169) (`LEVEL2_DASHBOARD_ACCESS`, `isAuthorizedForAnyRule`) and extended [`navigation.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/shared/types/navigation.ts#L17-L50) (`permissions`, `excludedRoles`, `authMode`).
  - Updated [`routes.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/routes.ts#L59-L122), [`guards.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/guards.ts#L46-L119), and [`AppLayout.vue`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/layouts/AppLayout.vue#L131-L190) (`isNavAuthorized` lines 429–453) to share the exact TD-05 / `GetExecutiveDashboardHandler.cs` OR-semantics rules and `excludedRoles: ['Student', 'Parent']` safeguard.
  - Added 8 Level 2 authorization test cases in [`guards.spec.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/router/__tests__/guards.spec.ts#L509-L802) (33/33 passed) and sidebar parity assertions in [`AppLayout.spec.ts`](file:///d:/Project.Private/elsasa-sis-v3/src/frontend/src/app/layouts/__tests__/AppLayout.spec.ts#L550-L624) (26/26 passed).

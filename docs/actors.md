# Actor Definition — Elsasa SIS V3

## 1. Actor Catalog

| Actor | Responsibility | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Applicant** | Prospective learner seeking admission; provides personal identity information, prior academic history, and required application credentials; sits for diagnostic entrance assessments or admissions interviews. | Submits formal admission application; accepts or declines an official offer of admission; withdraws an active application. |
| **Student** | Enrolled active learner; participates in scheduled learning activities; submits coursework tasks and assessments; engages in academic interventions and wellbeing support; joins approved extracurricular activities; confirms annual re-enrollment intent. | Submits assigned coursework and assessment evidence; views authorized academic feedback, grades, transcripts, and financial statements; requests reassessment or record corrections where policy permits; self-refers for non-academic or counselling support. |
| **Parent / Guardian** | Legal representative of an applicant or enrolled student; provides certified identity, medical, and contact details; receives official institutional communications, billing notices, and progress reports; collaborates on intervention plans. | Submits admission applications and confirms enrollment on behalf of minor applicants; authorizes administrative lifecycle transitions (leaves of absence, external transfers, voluntary withdrawals); authorizes extracurricular participation; provides legal consent for specialized counselling or developmental support when required; views billing statements and executes fee payments. |
| **Payer** | Individual or entity (parent, student, external sponsor, employer, embassy, or scholarship foundation) assuming financial liability for assessed obligations; transfers funds to the school via authorized payment channels; provides verifiable payment evidence. | Executes payments toward student accounts; receives official payment receipts; receives disbursed refunds of verified credit balances or overpayments. |
| **External Payment Provider** | External banking institution, gateway service, or electronic payment processor; processes financial transactions initiated by payers. | Transmits authoritative payment processing outcomes (transaction initiation, clearance, failure, settlement, or reversal notices) to the SIS. |
| **Admission Officer** | Administers prospective student intake operations; publishes admission campaigns; registers applications and verifies documentary completeness; coordinates entrance testing and interview logistics with Academics. | Determines admissibility of applicants against established admission criteria; records official admission decisions (Accepted, Rejected, Waitlisted, Incomplete); prepares accepted applicants for enrollment handover. |
| **Student Administration Officer** | Administers official student records and administrative lifecycle transitions; assigns unique Student Numbers; maintains certified biographical and guardian data; manages administrative placements (campus, level, cohort, homeroom); processes annual re-enrollments; coordinates dual clearance prerequisites for external transfers and graduation. | Executes enrollment and activates official Student status upon acceptance and payment gate satisfaction; executes administrative promotions, retentions, leaves of absence, returns from leave, internal transfers, external transfers-out, withdrawals, and graduations in accordance with authorized approvals and clearances; updates official student placement. |
| **School Leadership** | Executive institutional head and leadership team (Principal, Head of School, Director, Registrar); provides executive governance and policy oversight across school operations; oversees institutional compliance and safety. | Grants final institutional approval for exceptional admissions, student leaves of absence, multi-campus transfers, external transfer releases, voluntary withdrawals, and graduation conferrals; authorizes disciplinary expulsion status transitions upon recommendation; authorizes emergency school/campus closures; executes mandatory administrative payment overrides for applicant enrollment (GAP-01); approves institutional policies across domains. |
| **Teacher** | Delivers planned instructional sessions and learning activities; takes and records classroom and statutory homeroom attendance (GAP-03); designs, issues, and tracks coursework assignments; administers assessments and evaluates student work; identifies underperformance and provides classroom support; reports student behavioural incidents and positive observations; declares availability constraints; conducts handover at assignment completion. | Records observed attendance; issues assignments and assessment tasks; scores student work, provides evaluative feedback, and records provisional academic evaluations; nominates students for academic interventions or non-academic recognitions; declares individual scheduling availability; requests substitute coverage. |
| **Academic Coordinator** | Coordinates curriculum frameworks, learning structures, learning units, and syllabi; formulates individualized Student Learning Plans (SLP); registers students into learning units; coordinates assessment schedules and examination administration; compiles cohort academic progress, report cards, and transcripts; evaluates routine academic clearance obligations; aggregates curriculum staffing demand for Teaching. | Establishes learning units and curriculum structures within approved frameworks; drafts and adjusts Student Learning Plans; registers active students into learning units; prepares cohort progression recommendations (promote/retain); determines routine Academic Clearance for transfer-out; nominates students for academic honors and distinctions. |
| **Academic Authority** | Institutional authority governing academic standards, curriculum policies, grading integrity, and graduation credentials (e.g. Academic Committee, Curriculum Director, Academic Head). | Formally approves curriculum structures, learning frameworks, and syllabus versions; approves initial Student Learning Plans and formal accommodations/exemptions; approves finalized grades and authorizes official result publication; approves academic standing and progression recommendations; certifies official Graduation Eligibility; approves post-publication grade corrections and transcript amendments; adjudicates exceptional academic clearance cases and clearance corrections; authorizes recognized prior learning credits for transfer-in students (GAP-08); approves academic achievement conferrals. |
| **Finance Officer** | Executes routine financial administration and student account bookkeeping; assesses standard fee charges (application, enrollment, tuition); compiles and distributes billing invoices and statements; receives, verifies, and confirms payments; allocates payments against outstanding obligations; tracks accounts receivable aging; prepares installment proposals and refund requests; verifies financial clearance status. | Assesses charges based on approved fee policies; confirms payment receipt evidence; executes standard payment allocations and credit applications; issues billing statements and reminders; verifies financial clearance status for transfer-out and graduation within policy thresholds; confirms enrollment fee payment status to unblock student intake. |
| **Finance Authority** | Institutional authority governing financial governance, fiscal policy, fee schedules, and fiscal adjudication (e.g. Bursar, Finance Director, Finance Committee). | Establishes and amends financial policies, charge catalogs, billing schedules, and currency rounding rules; approves scholarships, tuition discounts, concessions, and waivers; approves formal installment payment agreements; adjudicates billing disputes and authorizes binding fee adjustments, obligation cancellations, or write-offs; authorizes monetary refund disbursements; grants official Financial Clearance (and clearance corrections) for transfer-out and graduation; authorizes corrections to approved financial ledger records. |
| **Operations Coordinator** | Coordinates shared physical facilities, rooms, timetables, school calendars, and logistical operations; receives and triages activity requests; evaluates room capacity and scheduling constraints; creates scheduled occurrences and allocates rooms; records facility maintenance restrictions; manages schedule relocations, postponements, and cancellations; coordinates event logistics; formats and broadcasts official operational announcements. | Drafts calendar entries and operational periods; commits room allocations and publishes timetable occurrences; resolves routine room collisions and schedule adjustments; flags room restrictions and unavailabilities; broadcasts approved announcements; closes completed operational arrangements. |
| **Operations Authority** | Institutional authority governing operational schedules, institutional calendar, campus facilities, and operational risk (e.g. Operations Director, Calendar Authority, School Operations Committee). | Approves academic year dates, term divisions, operational periods, and official school calendars; declares emergency or scheduled campus closures and calendar amendments; adjudicates high-priority operational resource and facility disputes; approves major school events and external facility usage; authorizes publication of critical operational announcements; authorizes auditable corrections to approved operations records. |
| **Teaching Coordinator** | Coordinates teaching resources, faculty profiles, and instructional capacity; collects and verifies qualification documents and licenses; reviews teacher availability; receives curriculum staffing demands from Academics; evaluates faculty eligibility and workload capacity; formulates teaching assignment proposals; arranges substitute coverage for absent teachers; monitors workload thresholds; oversees end-of-period assignment handovers. | Maintains teaching resource profiles; certifies qualification documentary validity against policy criteria; evaluates teacher capacity and matches candidates to demand; proposes teaching assignments; arranges routine substitute teachers within established policy parameters. |
| **Teaching Authority** | Institutional authority governing teacher professional standards, faculty authorizations, and instructional governance (e.g. Head of Faculty, Teaching Director, Faculty Review Board). | Approves the formal activation and modification of Teaching Profiles; formally grants, restricts, suspends, or revokes Teaching Authorizations; grants binding approval for Teaching Assignments to learning units and homerooms; authorizes exceptions to maximum teaching workload thresholds; approves exceptional teacher substitution arrangements; authorizes auditable corrections to approved teaching records. |
| **Student Development Officer** | Coordinates non-academic student development, pastoral support, and extracurricular programs; logs behaviour observations and recognitions; triages incoming behaviour incident reports between support and discipline; receives and routes non-academic referrals; formulates and reviews holistic wellbeing support plans and behavioral interventions; oversees extracurricular program offerings, registrations, and participation; administers non-academic achievement recognitions; issues cross-domain support recommendations. | Triages behaviour incidents; approves non-academic intervention plans and pastoral support accommodations; establishes approved extracurricular program charters; certifies student extracurricular participation and non-academic achievements; issues authorized support recommendations to Academics and Students. |
| **Counselor** | Provides specialized, confidential psychological, emotional, and social counseling support; conducts private counseling sessions; assesses student mental health and wellbeing concerns; coordinates external therapeutic or medical care referrals. | Exercises professional discretion in evaluating clinical urgency and conducting counseling intake; maintains exclusive access and ownership over restricted counseling session notes and case files (GAP-04); determines internal counseling care plans; issues sanitized, non-confidential accommodation recommendations to Academics. |
| **Discipline Authority** | Adjudicative body or officer governing student conduct, disciplinary hearings, and procedural fairness (e.g. Discipline Officer, Disciplinary Committee, Dean of Students). | Receives escalated misconduct reports; conducts formal disciplinary investigations, reviews evidence, and convenes hearings; determines factual findings of infraction; imposes disciplinary sanctions within approved policy limits (e.g. detention, loss of privileges, disciplinary probation); formally recommends severe administrative lifecycle sanctions (suspension, expulsion / disciplinary withdrawal) to School Leadership and Students domain (GAP-02); authorizes corrections to discipline case records. |

## 3. Resolved Architectural Questions

### 3.1 Segregation of Duties in Operational Finance
* **Question:** Does institutional financial policy mandate a formal segregation of duties between billing assessment (creating monetary charges) and payment confirmation (cashiering)? If mandated by financial audit regulations, must the consolidated Finance Officer actor be partitioned into distinct Billing Officer and Cashier actors?
* **Resolution (Pragmatic & Minimalist):** **Consolidated Finance Officer**.
  * Keep a single consolidated `Finance Officer` actor responsible for billing assessments, fee generation, payment confirmations, and receipt recording.
  * Do not introduce distinct `Billing Officer` and `Cashier` actors.
  * Enforce integrity via immutable audit logging (recording user identity, timestamps, and action payloads) on all charge issuances and payment receipts.

### 3.2 Discretionary Monetary Thresholds for Financial Adjustments and Refunds
* **Question:** What specific monetary thresholds or criteria govern whether a Finance Officer can independently record routine fee adjustments or refund disbursements versus requiring mandatory prior authorization from the Finance Authority?
* **Resolution (Explicit & Strict Separation):** **Zero Discretionary Spending**.
  * `Finance Officer` only prepares, validates, and submits adjustments, charge cancellations, write-offs, and refund disbursement requests.
  * `Finance Authority` must explicitly review and authorize **all** financial adjustments, charge waivers, and monetary refunds, regardless of amount.
  * Aligns with the core Finance domain approval model (`Finance Officer reviews -> Finance Authority approves -> Refund/Adjustment Executed`).

### 3.3 Student Self-Service Decision Authority vs. Parental Governance
* **Question:** Under what conditions (e.g., student reaching the legal age of majority or enrollment in adult secondary programs) is an enrolled Student authorized to independently initiate binding administrative lifecycle actions—specifically Leave of Absence (`STD-WF-06`), Internal Transfer (`STD-WF-09`), or Voluntary Withdrawal (`STD-WF-11`)—without mandatory Parent / Guardian signature or authorization?
* **Resolution (Explicit Administrative Flag):** **Explicit `is_independent` Status**.
  * By default, all lifecycle transitions (Leave, Transfer, Withdrawal) require explicit `Parent / Guardian` authorization.
  * Independent student self-service is permitted only when a student is explicitly flagged with `is_independent = true` on their official student record (e.g., verified legal age of majority, emancipated minor, or independent adult learner), verified and recorded by the `Student Administration Officer`.

### 3.4 Multi-Guardian Authority and Conflicting Directives
* **Question:** When multiple legal guardians are officially linked to a student record and issue conflicting administrative instructions (e.g. one guardian requests an external transfer-out or withdrawal while another guardian objects), what operational authority hierarchy or legal documentation rules determine which guardian directive is binding upon the Student Administration Officer?
* **Resolution (Primary Guardian Rule + Dispute Hold):** **Presumptive Primary Authority with Dispute Escalation**.
  * The designated `Primary Guardian` (`is_primary_contact = true`) holds presumptive operational authority for day-to-day administrative requests.
  * If any secondary registered legal guardian lodges a formal written dispute or conflicting directive, the `Student Administration Officer` must place the pending lifecycle action into an administrative **Pending Dispute / On Hold** state.
  * The action cannot be executed until mutual written guardian consent or legally certified court/custody documentation is reviewed and signed off by `School Leadership`.

### 3.5 Structural Independence of Disciplinary Decision vs. Appeal Authorities
* **Question:** Who holds the authoritative jurisdiction to adjudicate formal disciplinary appeals, and how is that appeal authority structurally separated from the original investigating Discipline Authority (Discipline Officer / Committee) to guarantee procedural due process without inventing undocumented executive roles?
* **Resolution (Minimalist Due Process):** **School Leadership Adjudicates Appeals**.
  * The original misconduct investigation, hearing, factual findings, and initial sanctions are conducted and determined by the `Discipline Authority`.
  * All formal disciplinary appeals are escalated to and adjudicated by `School Leadership` (Head of School, Principal, or Executive Board).
  * This guarantees complete structural separation of powers and procedural due process without creating redundant standing committees or undocumented roles.

### 3.6 Parental Consent and Mandatory Reporting Boundaries in Counselling
* **Question:** Under what student age thresholds, clinical risk criteria, or statutory legal jurisdictions is explicit Parent / Guardian consent mandatory before a Counselor may establish an ongoing therapeutic support plan or maintain restricted counselling notes (`DEV-WF-05`), versus circumstances where student confidentiality legally overrides parental disclosure?
* **Resolution (Explicit Consent Flag + Strict Note Confidentiality):** **Triage Autonomy, Policy-Driven Consent & Total Note Isolation**.
  * **Intake & Crisis:** Initial counselling intake, urgent triage, and immediate crisis stabilization never require prior parental consent.
  * **Ongoing Care Plan:** Ongoing therapeutic support plans record an explicit `parental_consent_status` (`not_required`, `requested`, `granted`, `waived_for_safety`) based on school policy and student independence status.
  * **Strict Confidentiality:** Clinical case notes and session records are **exclusively restricted** to the `Counselor` (and authorized safeguarding leads). Session notes are never exposed in student or parent portals.
  * **Mandatory Reporting / Safety Override:** If severe imminent risk of harm or child protection concerns are identified, confidentiality disclosure exceptions and statutory reporting immediately escalate to `School Leadership` and designated safeguarding officers per legal mandate.

### 3.7 Teacher Grade Publication Authority vs. Central Academic Moderation
* **Question:** What institutional policy boundaries govern whether a Teacher possesses final decision authority to publish routine classroom marks and assignment evaluations directly to students and parents, or whether all evaluative results require formal moderation and release approval by the Academic Authority before visibility?
* **Resolution (Formative vs. Summative Tiering):** **Autonomous Formative vs. Moderated Summative**.
  * **Routine Classroom / Formative:** `Teachers` possess autonomous authority to record and publish routine assignment scores, formative assessments, and developmental feedback directly to students and parents.
  * **Summative & Official Records:** Formal term-end grades, summative examinations, academic standing determinations, report cards, and official transcripts strictly require formal moderation and approval by the `Academic Authority` (or delegated Academic Coordinator) prior to publication.

### 3.8 Authorization Threshold for Emergency Faculty Substitution
* **Question:** Can a Teaching Coordinator unilaterally authorize and commit an emergency substitute teacher to an active instructional session (`TCH-WF-09`), or does institutional policy mandate case-by-case authorization from the Teaching Authority before a substitute faculty member may conduct a class?
* **Resolution (Unilateral Emergency within Authorized Pool):** **Pre-Authorized Pool Delegation**.
  * The `Teaching Coordinator` has unilateral authority to immediately assign and commit an emergency substitute teacher, provided the substitute is drawn from the pool of faculty holding an **Active Teaching Authorization** for that subject/grade level and the assignment does not breach workload thresholds.
  * Case-by-case approval from the `Teaching Authority` is strictly required only for exceptions: assigning a substitute who lacks active subject authorization, assigning an external/unvetted instructor, or granting an exception to maximum workload caps.

### 3.9 Operational Activity Cancellation and Rescheduling Autonomy
* **Question:** Does an operational request initiator (e.g. Teacher, Academic Coordinator, or Student Development Officer) possess independent authority to cancel or reschedule an approved room reservation and scheduled occurrence, or does all operational modification require formal triage and execution by the Operations Coordinator?
* **Resolution (Asymmetric Autonomy):** **Autonomous Cancellation, Centralized Rescheduling**.
  * **Cancellation (Releasing Resources):** The original request initiator (Teacher, Academic Coordinator, etc.) possesses autonomous authority to cancel their own approved reservation/occurrence, immediately releasing the room and facilities back to the operational pool and notifying participants.
  * **Rescheduling / Relocating (Re-allocating Resources):** Moving dates, times, or room assignments requires submission to and execution by the `Operations Coordinator` to prevent timetable collisions, room double-bookings, and downstream operational conflicts.

# Development Domain

## Purpose

The Development domain manages each student's non-academic growth, behaviour, wellbeing, counselling, discipline, extracurricular development, and non-academic achievements.

It is the authoritative source for:

* observed student behaviour and positive recognition
* formal discipline cases and their outcomes
* counselling and wellbeing support managed by the school
* non-academic referrals and interventions
* extracurricular participation and development
* non-academic achievements
* the history of authorized Development actions and decisions

The center of this domain is the student's development and support journey, not a collection of incidents.

This domain does not manage student identity or administrative status, academic learning or results, financial obligations, teacher resources, or operational schedules.

---

## Domain Responsibility

The Development domain owns:

* positive and concerning behaviour observations
* behaviour incidents
* discipline cases
* discipline determinations, actions, and appeals
* counselling referrals and cases
* wellbeing concerns and support
* non-academic intervention plans and reviews
* extracurricular programs from a developmental perspective
* extracurricular participation, roles, and outcomes
* non-academic achievements and recognition
* permitted Development recommendations to other domains
* Development record correction and history

Development is authoritative for the meaning and outcome of non-academic support and development work.

Other domains may submit referrals or consume permitted outcomes. They must not redefine Development records or gain access to confidential details merely because they participate in a related workflow.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

#### Behaviour Observation

Manage factual observations of student behaviour.

Observations may include:

* positive conduct
* concerning conduct
* interpersonal behaviour
* adherence to school expectations
* contextual information
* people directly involved
* immediate response
* supporting evidence
* follow-up need

An observation is not automatically an incident, policy violation, or discipline determination.

Records must distinguish observed facts from interpretation, allegation, and conclusion.

---

#### Positive Behaviour Recognition

Recognize conduct that reflects the school's approved values or development expectations.

Recognition may include:

* commendation
* character recognition
* leadership recognition
* service recognition
* positive contribution

Recognition criteria and approval authority must be explicit.

Positive recognition is not an academic result.

---

#### Behaviour Incident

Manage an event involving behaviour that may require review or response.

An incident may record:

* date, time, and location
* reporter
* students and other people involved
* factual description
* alleged policy concern
* available evidence
* immediate safety or support action
* notification and escalation
* review outcome

Reporting an incident does not establish that a student committed a violation.

Related reports may be linked without erasing their original source or wording.

---

#### Discipline Case

Manage the formal review of an alleged violation of school behaviour or discipline policy.

A discipline case may include:

* case initiation
* applicable policy version
* allegation
* evidence and statements
* student opportunity to respond
* authorized review
* determination
* corrective, restorative, or disciplinary action
* communication to authorized parties
* appeal or reconsideration
* follow-up and closure

The Development domain owns the discipline case and its authorized determination.

When an outcome requires an official administrative transition, Development provides an approved recommendation or decision reference to Students. Students executes the status change.

---

#### Counselling

Manage authorized counselling support provided or coordinated by the school.

Counselling may include:

* self-referral or referral by an authorized person
* presenting concern
* urgency assessment
* consent when required
* counselor assignment
* session occurrence
* counselling goals
* support actions
* review and follow-up
* external referral
* closure

Counselling content is sensitive. Access to session notes and disclosures must be more restrictive than access to ordinary referral status or permitted support outcomes.

Development must share the minimum information necessary for another actor to perform an authorized action.

---

#### Student Wellbeing

Manage school-based response to social, emotional, personal, or other non-academic wellbeing concerns.

Wellbeing work may include:

* concerns raised by the student
* observations or referrals from authorized people
* needs or risk review
* immediate support
* wellbeing support plan
* reasonable support recommendation
* family engagement when permitted
* internal or external referral
* review and closure

Development does not automatically own medical diagnosis, clinical treatment, or general health records.

Safeguarding and child-protection responsibilities require explicit school policy and legal clarification before implementation.

---

#### Development Referral

Manage requests for non-academic review or support.

A referral should identify:

* referring actor
* student reference
* reason and observed context
* urgency
* information permitted to be shared
* requested support, when known
* acceptance, redirection, or rejection
* assigned responsible actor
* permitted outcome returned to the referrer

A referral is a request for review. It is not proof of misconduct, a diagnosis, or an authorized conclusion.

---

#### Non-Academic Intervention

Manage planned action intended to improve or support behaviour, wellbeing, social participation, personal responsibility, or other non-academic development.

Interventions may include:

* behaviour improvement plan
* mentoring
* restorative action
* social integration support
* personal development plan
* attendance-related non-academic support
* family engagement
* coordinated student support

An intervention should identify:

* reason and evidence
* goals
* responsible actor
* planned actions
* start date and review date
* student and guardian involvement when applicable
* progress reviews
* outcome and closure reason

Academics owns interventions whose primary purpose is improving learning performance. Development owns non-academic interventions.

---

#### Extracurricular Program

Manage the developmental meaning and participation rules of organized non-academic activities.

Examples include:

* clubs
* sports
* arts
* student organizations
* leadership activities
* community service
* camps
* non-academic competitions

Development may define:

* purpose and expected development outcomes
* participation requirements
* eligibility rules approved by policy
* capacity or selection rules
* participant roles
* completion or recognition criteria

Operations owns calendar, event, room, and operational schedule information. Development consumes that context.

An activity formally assessed as part of the student's learning belongs to Academics even when it resembles an extracurricular activity.

---

#### Extracurricular Participation

Manage the student's involvement in an extracurricular program.

Participation may include:

* application, nomination, invitation, or assignment
* selection or approval
* active participation
* extracurricular attendance where required
* role or responsibility
* participation progress
* completion
* withdrawal or removal

Extracurricular participation is not administrative enrollment and is not academic participation.

General school-event attendance belongs to the domain that owns the event's business purpose. Learning-activity attendance belongs to Academics.

---

#### Non-Academic Achievement

Manage authorized recognition arising from extracurricular or other non-academic development.

Examples include:

* sports awards
* arts awards
* leadership recognition
* community-service recognition
* character awards
* extracurricular competition results
* approved participation certificates

An achievement should identify its category, source, evidence, date, approving authority, and publication status when relevant.

Academics owns academic achievements. Development owns extracurricular and other non-academic achievements.

---

#### Development History and Correction

Preserve authorized Development actions and outcomes over time.

History may include:

* observation recorded
* incident reported and reviewed
* discipline case opened, determined, appealed, and closed
* referral received and assigned
* counselling or wellbeing support opened and closed
* intervention established and reviewed
* extracurricular participation started or concluded
* achievement recognized
* recommendation issued
* approved record corrected

Approved records must not be silently overwritten.

Corrections should preserve the original record, corrected information, reason, authority, and time of correction.

### Academics

* learning structure and learning plan
* learning activities

* assignments and assessments
* grades and academic results
* learning progress
* academic intervention
* academic achievements

### Finance

* billing and receivables
* payment and allocation
* discount and refund
* scholarship decision and financial application
* financial penalties

### Teaching

* teacher profile
* teacher qualification
* teaching assignment
* teacher availability and workload

### Operations

* academic calendar
* school events as operational events
* announcements
* room allocation
* operational schedule
* cross-domain operational approvals

### Other Exclusions

* employee discipline or employee counselling
* payroll

## Actors

The exact actor names and authority may differ between schools.

### Student

May:

* request support
* provide information or a response
* participate in counselling or intervention
* join extracurricular activities
* review information when policy permits
* appeal or request reconsideration when permitted

A student's access to sensitive records, and any limitation on that access, must follow applicable policy and law.

### Parent or Guardian

May:

* raise a concern
* provide relevant information
* give consent when required and authorized
* participate in support planning
* receive permitted communication
* request review or appeal when policy permits

The official relationship and authority of the parent or guardian come from Students.

### Teacher or Learning Facilitator

May:

* record an observation
* report an incident or concern
* recognize positive behaviour
* submit a Development referral
* perform an assigned support action
* receive minimum necessary guidance

A teacher does not automatically gain access to counselling notes or the complete Development case.

### Student Advisor or Homeroom Advisor

May coordinate permitted day-to-day student support, observations, communication, and referrals.

The existence and authority of this role must be confirmed by school policy.

### Counselor

May:

* review counselling referrals
* assess urgency within authorized competence
* provide counselling support
* maintain restricted counselling records
* coordinate permitted referrals
* communicate minimum necessary outcomes

Professional and legal obligations must be defined before implementation.

### Student Development Officer

May:

* review Development referrals
* coordinate interventions
* monitor behaviour and development concerns
* manage extracurricular development
* issue permitted recommendations

### Discipline Officer or Committee

May:

* review discipline cases
* evaluate evidence and responses
* determine findings
* authorize actions within policy
* review appeals when authorized

The decision-maker and appeal authority must not be assumed to be the same actor.

### Extracurricular Coordinator or Facilitator

May:

* manage program participation
* record required participation information
* review development outcomes
* recommend non-academic recognition

Operational scheduling and room allocation remain with Operations.

### School Leadership

May approve specified discipline actions, interventions, recognition, disclosures, or administrative recommendations according to policy.

Leadership access to confidential details must still be purpose-based and authorized.

### Safeguarding Officer

May receive and act on safeguarding concerns if this responsibility is explicitly included.

No safeguarding workflow, access rule, or reporting duty may be invented. Applicable school policy and law must be established first.

## Core Business Concepts

### Behaviour Observation

A dated factual record of positive or concerning behaviour in a specific context.

### Behaviour Incident

An event reported for review because behaviour may require support, investigation, or response.

### Discipline Case

A formal, policy-governed review of an alleged behaviour violation.

### Development Referral

A request for the Development domain to review or support a student.

### Counselling Case

A restricted record of authorized counselling support and its management history.

### Wellbeing Case

A coordinated school response to an identified non-academic wellbeing concern.

### Intervention Plan

An authorized set of non-academic goals, actions, responsibilities, and reviews for a student.

### Extracurricular Program

An organized non-academic activity intended to provide participation or development opportunities.

### Extracurricular Participation

The student's authorized involvement, role, and participation history in an extracurricular program.

### Non-Academic Achievement

Authorized recognition of extracurricular, character, leadership, service, or other non-academic development.

### Development Recommendation

An authorized Development conclusion provided to another domain for that domain's decision or execution.

### Confidentiality Classification

The approved access and disclosure treatment applicable to a Development record.

Classification does not replace actor, purpose, consent, legal, or case-specific authorization checks.

### Development Record Correction

An authorized, auditable correction that preserves the original Development record and correction reason.

## Student Development Journey

A conceptual Development journey may include:

```text
Observation, Self-Request, Referral, or Program Opportunity
                        │
                        ▼
                  Initial Review
                        │
          ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
          ▼             ▼             ▼
      No Action      Support       Formal Review
       Needed        Pathway          Pathway
                         │              │
             ┌───────────┼──────┐       ▼
             ▼           ▼      ▼   Determination
        Counselling  Wellbeing  Intervention
             │           │      │       │
             └───────────┴──────┴───────┘
                         │
                         ▼
                  Review and Outcome
                         │
                         ▼
                 Closure or Follow-up

Extracurricular Opportunity
            │
            ▼
       Participation
            │
            ▼
 Development, Completion, or Recognition
```

This is not a mandatory state machine.

Not every observation becomes a referral. Not every referral becomes a case. Not every incident becomes a discipline finding. Support may occur without discipline, and extracurricular participation may proceed independently of concern-management workflows.

## Main Workflows

## 1. Record Behaviour Observation or Recognition

### Trigger

Authorized positive or concerning behaviour is observed.

### Actor

* Teacher or Learning Facilitator
* Student Advisor
* Student Development Officer
* Other authorized school actor

### Input

* student identity reference
* date, location, and context
* factual observation
* people involved
* immediate response
* available evidence

### Outcome

* observation or positive recognition is recorded
* follow-up need is identified

### Business Rules

* facts, interpretations, and allegations must remain distinguishable
* observation must not automatically establish a policy violation
* only necessary personal information may be recorded
* correction history must be preserved

---

## 2. Report and Triage Behaviour Incident

### Trigger

An event may require safety action, support, or formal review.

### Actor

* Authorized reporter
* Student Development Officer
* Discipline Officer

### Input

* incident report
* involved people
* immediate risk or support information
* available evidence
* applicable context

### Outcome

* immediate needs are addressed or escalated
* report is accepted, redirected, linked, or closed with reason
* support or formal discipline review may be initiated

### Business Rules

* safety action must not be delayed merely because fact-finding is incomplete
* reporting does not establish guilt
* duplicate reports must not destroy independent source history
* safeguarding escalation must follow explicit policy

---

## 3. Investigate and Determine Discipline Case

### Trigger

An authorized review determines that an alleged policy violation requires a formal case.

### Actor

* Discipline Officer or Committee
* School Leadership, when required
* Authorized appeal authority

### Input

* incident information
* applicable policy version
* evidence and statements
* student response
* relevant prior information permitted by policy

### Outcome

* allegation is substantiated, unsubstantiated, inconclusive, or otherwise concluded under policy
* authorized action or support is recorded
* appeal availability is communicated where required

### Business Rules

* determination must identify evidence, policy, authority, and effective date
* the student must have the response opportunity required by policy
* prior history must not substitute for evidence about the current allegation
* an administrative status change must be executed by Students
* appeal must preserve the original decision and subsequent outcome

---

## 4. Receive and Route Development Referral

### Trigger

A student or authorized actor requests non-academic review or support.

### Actor

* Student
* Parent or Guardian
* Teacher or Student Advisor
* Student Development Officer
* Counselor

### Input

* referral reason
* observed context
* urgency
* requested support
* sharing permission or authority

### Outcome

* referral is acknowledged
* urgency is reviewed
* referral is accepted, redirected, or closed with reason
* responsible actor and permitted response are identified

### Business Rules

* referral is not a diagnosis or discipline finding
* sensitive information must not be copied into unrelated records
* urgent risk must follow established escalation policy
* referrer receives only information authorized for the referral purpose

---

## 5. Manage Counselling Support

### Trigger

An accepted counselling referral or student request requires support.

### Actor

* Counselor
* Student
* Parent or Guardian, when authorized

### Input

* referral or request
* consent when required
* presenting concern
* urgency and relevant context

### Outcome

* counselling support is opened, provided, reviewed, referred, or closed
* permitted support outcome is communicated where necessary

### Business Rules

* counselling notes require restricted access
* session occurrence and session content may require different access rules
* disclosure must follow consent, safety, professional, legal, and school-policy requirements
* other domains should consume actionable outcomes rather than detailed notes
* the system must not invent clinical assessments or treatment rules

---

## 6. Assess and Support Wellbeing Concern

### Trigger

A wellbeing concern is reported or identified.

### Actor

* Counselor
* Student Development Officer
* Student Advisor
* Safeguarding Officer, when applicable

### Input

* concern or referral
* current context
* permitted relevant history
* immediate needs
* consent or authority

### Outcome

* concern is reviewed
* immediate support, plan, monitoring, or referral is established
* outcome and follow-up are recorded

### Business Rules

* access and disclosure must be purpose-based
* school-based support must not be represented as medical diagnosis
* risk categories and escalation actions must come from explicit policy
* administrative, academic, or financial effects remain decisions of their owning domains

---

## 7. Establish and Review Non-Academic Intervention

### Trigger

An authorized review identifies a non-academic development or support need.

### Actor

* Student Development Officer
* Counselor
* Student Advisor
* Authorized support team

### Input

* reason and evidence
* student needs and strengths
* authorized goals
* available support actions
* responsible actors

### Outcome

* intervention plan is established
* actions and review dates are assigned
* progress is reviewed
* plan is continued, revised, completed, referred, or closed

### Business Rules

* every plan must have an owner, purpose, review date, and outcome
* goals and actions must be understandable to authorized participants
* academic interventions remain owned by Academics
* lack of improvement must not automatically trigger an administrative status change
* revisions and closure must preserve history

---

## 8. Establish Extracurricular Program

### Trigger

An approved non-academic development opportunity will be offered.

### Actor

* Extracurricular Coordinator
* Student Development Officer
* School Leadership, when approval is required

### Input

* purpose and development outcomes
* participation and eligibility policy
* capacity or selection rules
* operational requirements
* recognition or completion criteria

### Outcome

* extracurricular program is approved for participation
* operational needs are requested from Operations

### Business Rules

* Development owns program purpose and participation meaning
* Operations owns event, calendar, room, and schedule context
* Teaching assignment must not be inferred from facilitating an activity
* academic assessment makes the relevant learning activity an Academics responsibility

---

## 9. Manage Extracurricular Participation

### Trigger

A student applies, is nominated, invited, selected, or assigned under approved policy.

### Actor

* Student
* Parent or Guardian, when required
* Extracurricular Coordinator or Facilitator

### Input

* student identity and status context
* participation request or nomination
* eligibility and capacity rules
* required consent

### Outcome

* participation is approved, waitlisted, rejected, started, completed, withdrawn, or removed
* roles, required attendance, and development outcomes are preserved

### Business Rules

* eligibility must follow explicit policy
* participation must not change official student status
* selection and removal reasons must be recorded when policy requires
* learning-activity attendance must not be duplicated as extracurricular attendance

---

## 10. Recognize Non-Academic Achievement

### Trigger

A student's non-academic development or accomplishment is proposed for recognition.

### Actor

* Extracurricular Facilitator
* Student Development Officer
* Authorized approving authority

### Input

* achievement category
* evidence and source
* applicable recognition criteria
* achievement date

### Outcome

* achievement is approved, rejected, published, or corrected
* authorized achievement history is preserved

### Business Rules

* recognition must identify its evidence and authority
* Development must not duplicate academic achievement
* external claims must retain their source and verification status
* publication and correction must follow explicit authority

---

## 11. Issue Cross-Domain Recommendation

### Trigger

An authorized Development outcome requires action or consideration by another domain.

### Actor

* Student Development Officer
* Discipline authority
* Counselor or wellbeing authority, when permitted
* School Leadership

### Input

* authorized Development outcome
* recommendation purpose
* minimum supporting information
* effective date and authority

### Outcome

* recommendation or decision reference is provided to the owning domain
* receiving domain's response may be recorded

### Business Rules

* recommendation must not directly execute another domain's decision
* confidential source details must not be disclosed unless required and authorized
* Students owns administrative status execution
* Academics owns academic decisions
* Finance owns financial decisions

---

## 12. Correct Approved Development Record

### Trigger

An approved Development record is found to be inaccurate or incomplete.

### Actor

* Authorized Development Officer
* Discipline authority
* Counselor, for authorized counselling records
* School Leadership, when required

### Input

* original record
* requested correction
* reason and evidence
* authorization

### Outcome

* corrected information becomes effective
* original information and correction history remain auditable
* affected outcomes and disclosures can be identified

### Business Rules

* approved records must never be silently overwritten
* correction authority must respect record sensitivity and ownership
* correction is distinct from appeal or changed professional judgment
* affected recommendations must be reviewed

## Business Rules

### Student Development First

Design around the student's growth, safety, support, participation, and restoration. Do not design Development merely as a punishment register.

### Fact, Allegation, and Determination

Observed fact, reported allegation, supporting evidence, professional assessment, and authorized determination are different concepts and must remain distinguishable.

### Support and Discipline Separation

A student may need support without discipline. A discipline case may also result in support. One must not be used as an unrestricted substitute for the other.

### Confidentiality by Purpose

Access depends on the actor, purpose, record type, consent, policy, and applicable law.

Do not assume that all Development staff, teachers, guardians, or school leaders may view every Development record.

### Minimum Necessary Disclosure

Share the actionable information another actor needs, not the complete case merely for convenience.

### Administrative Status Separation

Development may issue an authorized recommendation or decision reference. Students executes suspension-related status changes, transfer, withdrawal, or other official lifecycle transitions.

### Academic Separation

Development may consume permitted academic context and provide support outcomes. It must not change attendance, grades, results, learning progress, academic standing, or academic intervention ownership.

### Financial Separation

Development may provide permitted evidence or recommendations. Finance owns charges, penalties, discounts, scholarships, refunds, and payment decisions.

### Extracurricular Boundary

Development owns non-academic purpose, participation, development outcome, and recognition. Operations owns schedule, event, and room context. Academics owns formally assessed learning.

### Policy and Authority Are Explicit

Discipline categories, consequences, appeals, counselling disclosure, wellbeing escalation, eligibility, recognition, and safeguarding actions must come from approved policy.

Agents must never invent them.

### Historical Integrity

Preserve reports, evidence, decisions, plans, reviews, appeals, disclosures, recommendations, and corrections.

### Effective Dating

The date information is recorded may differ from the event date, decision date, and effective date. These meanings must remain distinguishable.

### No Automatic Adverse Decision

Do not infer misconduct, risk, punishment, exclusion, suspension, transfer, or withdrawal solely from:

* one observation or referral
* an allegation
* counselling participation
* a wellbeing concern
* academic difficulty
* financial status
* a historical case
* absence of a recorded follow-up

### No Silent Deletion

Closing, withdrawing, correcting, or restricting a record must not silently erase required business history.

Retention and lawful deletion rules remain policy questions.

### Minimum Required Information

Collect only information required for the current authorized workflow. Sensitive information must not be gathered solely because it might be useful later.

## Inputs from Other Domains

### From Students

* student identity reference
* parent or guardian relationship and authority
* enrollment and active-status context
* administrative placement
* leave, transfer, withdrawal, and graduation events

Development references this information but does not redefine it.

### From Academics

* academic concern referral
* permitted learning attendance or progress context
* request for non-academic support
* academic intervention status when relevant and authorized

Development must not copy complete academic records when a limited context is sufficient.

### From Teaching

* teacher identity reference
* teaching assignment context
* teacher-originated observation or referral

Development does not own teacher identity, assignment, availability, or workload.

### From Operations

* academic year and calendar context
* school event context
* operational schedule
* room allocation
* operational approval context

### From Finance

* only authorized financial context required by a specific support or eligibility workflow

Financial difficulty may be referral context but Development must not redefine financial standing.

## Outputs to Other Domains

### To Students

* approved administrative recommendation related to behaviour or wellbeing
* discipline decision reference required for authorized administrative execution
* Development clearance or condition only when explicit policy requires it

Students decides or executes official administrative transitions within its authority.

### To Academics

* permitted support outcome
* actionable accommodation or support recommendation
* referral status relevant to academic coordination
* non-academic participation or achievement context when authorized

Detailed counselling notes, discipline evidence, and sensitive wellbeing records must not be copied merely for convenience.

### To Teaching

* minimum necessary classroom or student-support guidance
* permitted referral outcome
* assigned Development-support action

### To Operations

* extracurricular schedule, event, room, or resource request
* permitted operational restriction or support requirement
* Development participation requirements relevant to coordination

### To Finance

* approved non-academic scholarship recommendation or supporting recognition only when policy requires it
* permitted support context for a defined financial workflow

Finance owns the resulting scholarship, discount, charge, refund, or payment decision.

## Reporting Responsibility

Development may provide operational information such as:

* referrals awaiting triage or assignment
* incidents awaiting review
* discipline cases by stage and required action
* interventions due for review
* permitted behaviour trends
* counselling or wellbeing service workload using appropriately restricted information
* extracurricular applications, capacity, and participation
* extracurricular attendance where Development owns it
* non-academic achievements and recognition status
* recommendations awaiting response from another domain
* correction and appeal workload

Reports must be produced from actual Development workflows.

Do not create separate data-entry processes solely for dashboards.

Sensitive reports must use minimum necessary detail. Aggregate reporting must still consider re-identification risk and access authority.

Cross-domain dashboards may combine Development information with Students, Academics, Finance, Teaching, or Operations information, but ownership and confidentiality must remain explicit.

## Guiding Principles

### Development Journey First

Design around what helps the student grow, participate, recover, and receive appropriate support.

### Strengths and Concerns

Support positive development and achievements as well as concerns and incidents.

### Fair Process

Separate report, evidence, response, determination, action, and appeal.

### Privacy Is a Business Rule

Confidentiality and disclosure are part of the workflow, not merely technical permissions added later.

### Support Without Stigma

Counselling, wellbeing support, or intervention must not automatically label a student or imply misconduct.

### Recommendation Is Not Execution

Development provides its authorized conclusion. The owning domain performs its own decision or transition.

### Preserve Development History

Keep reports, support, decisions, participation, recognition, disclosure, and corrections interpretable over time.

### Actor-Centric Workspaces

Show each actor what needs attention, what action is authorized, what information may be accessed, and what outcome is expected.

### Simple Before Flexible

Implement the smallest workflows and classifications required by the current school. Do not create a speculative universal case-management or clinical system.

* Which decisions may be appealed, by whom, and to whom?
* Are suspension and expulsion Development decisions or recommendations to Students?
* How are restorative actions represented?

* When is guardian consent required?

* Which disclosures are permitted or required?

* Which wellbeing concerns are in scope?
* Does the domain include health-office or medical information?
* Does Development own safeguarding and child-protection cases?
* Which risk categories and escalation rules apply?
* What legal reporting duties exist?

### Non-Academic Intervention

* What triggers an intervention?
* Who creates, approves, participates in, and closes a plan?
* How are goals and outcomes evaluated?
* When should Development refer a concern to Academics or an external provider?

### Attendance Boundary

* Does Development own general daily school attendance or only extracurricular attendance?
* Does Operations own attendance at general school events?
* How are attendance-related wellbeing concerns referred without duplicating Academics records?

### Extracurricular Development

* Who approves programs and participation rules?
* How are capacity, selection, waiting lists, and removal handled?
* Which activities are extracurricular versus formally academic?
* Who records participation and attendance?

## Implementation Guardrails

Before implementing a Development feature:

1. Identify the student development, support, participation, or fair-process outcome.
2. Identify the actor, authority, and purpose for access.
3. Classify the record as observation, allegation, evidence, assessment, determination, support, or recognition.
4. Identify the applicable policy version and effective date.
5. Determine whether consent, notification, escalation, or restricted disclosure is required.
6. Identify information owned by Students, Academics, Finance, Teaching, or Operations.
7. Identify the minimum output and which actor or domain may consume it.
8. Preserve history, decisions, reviews, appeals, disclosures, and corrections.
9. Confirm that the workflow supports both positive development and concerns where applicable.
10. Implement the minimum workflow.

Do not begin from:

* database tables
* CRUD screens
* API endpoints
* a generic case-management product
* a punishment-point system
* a counselling-notes form
* a dashboard requirement
* unrestricted role-based access assumptions

Begin from the student's Development workflow and the actor's authorized purpose.

Do not invent:

* behaviour categories
* discipline violations or consequences
* points, demerits, or automatic sanctions
* investigation or appeal authority
* counselling consent or disclosure rules
* risk scoring or safeguarding escalation
* intervention goals or outcome formulas
* extracurricular eligibility and selection rules
* achievement categories or approval authority
* retention or deletion periods
* cross-domain access to sensitive information

When policy or workflow is unclear, record it as an open question and stop before encoding the assumption as a business rule.

For every proposed concept, ask:

```text
Does this describe non-academic growth, behaviour, support,
wellbeing, counselling, discipline, extracurricular participation,
or non-academic recognition?

If not, which domain owns it?

Is the information factual, alleged, assessed, or determined?

Who is authorized to know it, and for what purpose?

Is it required by a real workflow now?
```

## Summary

The Development domain answers these primary business questions:

```text
What positive or concerning behaviour has been observed?

Does a reported incident require support or formal review?

What fair and authorized discipline outcome was determined?

What counselling or wellbeing support does the student need?

What non-academic intervention is active, and is it helping?

Which extracurricular activities is the student participating in?

What non-academic development or achievement has been recognized?

What minimum authorized outcome should another domain receive?
```

It does not answer:

```text
Who is the student or what is the student's official status?

What is the student learning or what academic result was achieved?

Who is available or assigned to teach?

Which room, event, calendar, or operational schedule applies?

What does the student owe or which payment was made?
```

Those questions belong to Students, Academics, Teaching, Operations, and Finance respectively.
